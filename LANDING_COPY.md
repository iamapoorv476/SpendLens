## Hero Headline (≤10 words)

> Your AI tool spend, audited honestly.

7 words. Positions the product as trustworthy and direct rather than
hype-driven. "Honestly" does more work than "instantly" or "easily"
because the target user (technical founder, engineering manager) is
skeptical of tools that overpromise.

> Free audit for startups and engineering teams. No login.
> Results in 60 seconds. Every recommendation is explainable.

24 words. Addresses the three biggest objections upfront:
cost (free), friction (no login), and trust (explainable).

## FAQ — 5 Real Q&As

**Q1: Is this actually free?**
Yes. SpendLens is completely free to use. You do not need an account
or a credit card. Email is only asked for after you see your results —
never before. The tool is free because it is built by Credex as a
lead generation asset. If your audit shows significant savings, Credex
can help you capture more through discounted AI infrastructure credits.

**Q2: How accurate are the recommendations?**
Every pricing number in the engine traces to an official vendor pricing
page with a verification date. The logic is deterministic — the same
input always produces the same output. Each recommendation includes a
confidence level (high, medium, or low) and a one-sentence reason that
a finance person can verify independently. We do not manufacture savings.
If your stack is already optimal, we tell you that.

**Q3: Do you store my data?**
Your audit inputs and results are saved to generate your shareable URL.
Identifying details (email, company name) are stripped from the public
version of your audit. We do not sell your data. If you submit your
email, it is stored securely in our database and used only to send
your report and — for high-savings cases — to have a Credex team member
reach out.

**Q4: What tools do you cover?**
SpendLens currently audits: Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT,
Anthropic API, OpenAI API, Gemini, and Windsurf. These cover the
majority of AI tool spend for technical teams in 2026. Pricing is
verified weekly against official vendor pages.

**Q5: What is Credex and why did they build this?**
Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits — Cursor, Claude,
ChatGPT Enterprise, and others — sourced from companies that
overforecast or pivoted. The discount is real and substantial. SpendLens
is a free tool that helps any startup identify where they are
overspending. For teams with significant savings opportunities, Credex
is the natural next step. You get a free audit. Credex gets a qualified
lead. Both sides win.
